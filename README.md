# Sales Analysis

## 1. Business Problem

Analyze sales performance to understand revenue,
transactions, customer behavior, and product performance.

## 2. Business Questions

- What is the total revenue?
- Which stores generate the highest revenue?
- Which products contribute the most revenue?
- How does sales performance change over time?
- Which stores or products have weak performance?

## 3. KPIs

- Total Revenue
- Total Transactions
- Average Bill
- Units Sold
- Profit
- Profit Margin
